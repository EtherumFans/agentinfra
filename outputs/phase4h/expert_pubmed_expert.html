Expert Overview

The PubMed Expert connects AI agents to PubMed, providing access to a vast corpus of peer-reviewed biomedical literature, including abstracts and citations from MEDLINE, life science journals, and authoritative research databases.

This expert is designed for workflows that require evidence-based grounding in primary scientific literature. Agents can retrieve published studies, clinical research findings, and mechanistic evidence to support clinical reasoning, education, and analysis without relying on secondary summaries alone.

By integrating PubMed directly into agent workflows, teams can anchor outputs in peer-reviewed research, improve transparency of evidence sources, and support rigorous, auditable use of biomedical literature across clinical, educational, and research-oriented use cases.

Capabilities

  • Retrieve peer-reviewed biomedical research articles and abstracts
  • Surface primary evidence from clinical and translational studies
  • Support literature-based clinical reasoning and education
  • Enable evidence-backed analysis for research and guideline evaluation workflow

Example Use Cases‍

  • Evidence-based clinical support: Retrieve relevant studies to contextualize mechanisms, outcomes, or emerging evidence during clinical analysis or guideline review.
  • Medical education and research training: Surface primary literature to help students, trainees, or researchers understand how evidence is generated and interpreted.
  • Scientific validation and auditability: Provide traceable, peer-reviewed sources to support claims, reasoning steps, or educational explanations in agent outputs
